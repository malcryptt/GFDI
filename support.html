Every contribution fuels our fight against female genital mutilation, child labour, and human trafficking — and helps us build a safer, fairer Nigeria for women and children.

Why Your Support Matters

Gender Frontier Development Initiatives (GFDI) is a grassroots advocacy organization committed to defending the human rights of vulnerable women and children in Nigeria. From campaigning against harmful traditional practices to intervening in crisis situations, our work is powered by people like you.

Protect Children

Help us end child labour, human trafficking, and female genital mutilation in vulnerable communities across Nigeria.

Empower Women

Fund outreach, education, and advocacy that gives women the autonomy to shape their own futures without fear.

Drive Change

Support community development projects, entrepreneurship programmes, and grassroots interventions that create lasting impact.
