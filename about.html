Who We Are

Gender Frontier Development Initiatives (GFDI) is a grassroots advocacy organization committed to defending the human rights of vulnerable women and children in Nigeria. By confronting systemic abuse and harmful traditional practices head-on, we work to protect those most at risk in our communities.

Our campaigns directly challenge female genital mutilation, child labour, and human trafficking — bringing awareness, intervening in crisis situations, and promoting education as a pathway to empowerment. We believe that every child deserves a safe environment to grow, and every woman deserves the autonomy to shape her own future without fear of violence or exploitation.

Principal Partner & Founder

Michael Olushola Ogunsakin

Michael Olushola Ogunsakin is the Principal Partner of Michael O. Ogunsakin & Co., an international law firm. He is a Rotarian and a life member of the Nigeria Society of International Law, as well as a member of the International Association of Human Right Lawyers, Red Cross, the Nigerian Bar Association (NBA), and the International Bar Association (IBA).

He is the Founder & Chairman of the Board of Trustees of Gender Frontier Initiative Developments, a Non-Governmental Organization promoting and advocating for Women's and Children's rights, campaigning against Female Genital Mutilation, Child Labour, and Human Trafficking. He is also keen on assisting the less privileged in society by engaging them in community developmental projects and entrepreneurship. A Motivational Speaker and expert in business process invention and re-engineering, he holds the responsibility of driving GFDI's Corporate Strategies.
