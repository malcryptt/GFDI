Get Involved

On-the-ground advocacy requires people and partnerships. Join GFDI's network by volunteering your time or collaborating as an institutional partner.

What Volunteering Involves

GFDI volunteers work alongside our activists in a range of areas — from direct field outreach in communities, to providing legal-aid escort for survivors, to handling communications and fundraising that keeps our work going. All volunteers are onboarded with a briefing on GFDI's mission, safeguarding policies, and the communities we serve. You set your own availability.

If you have questions before applying, feel free to contact us first.
