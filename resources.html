Emergency Resources

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please use the following contacts. In a non-emergency, you can report a case to GFDI here.

Are you in immediate danger?

Contact the Nigerian Police Force emergency lines or use one of the specialized agency hotlines below. Do not wait for an online report to be processed if physical safety is currently at risk.

Important Hotlines

  • National Emergency Number
    112

    0800 CALL NAPTIP  ·  Short code: 627

Educational Information

About Female Genital Mutilation (FGM)

FGM involves the partial or total removal of external female genitalia or other injury to the female genital organs for non-medical reasons. It is a severe violation of the human rights of girls and women, with no health benefits. It can cause severe bleeding, problems urinating, cysts, infections, and complications in childbirth.

Recognizing Human Trafficking

Trafficking often starts with an offer that seems to be a genuine opportunity for education or work. Key signs include: living with an employer in poor conditions, inability to speak to others alone, lack of access to personal identification documents, and signs of physical or psychological abuse. Do not attempt a direct rescue; report suspicions immediately.
